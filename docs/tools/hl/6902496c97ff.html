" A grammar is data. A parsing expression is one of " " \"text\" the text, after blanks and comments " " Tight{\"text\"} the text, without skipping blanks " " Key{\"word\"} the word, not followed by a letter, a digit or _ " " name the rule name (a symbol) " " f a function: f(p) returns the new position or -1, and sets peg_pv " " Seq{e, ...} sequence. The value is Tuple{v, ...} " " Choice{e, ...} ordered choice " " Many{e}, Many1{e}, Option{e} the values are Tuple{v, ...}, Option gives Tuple{} or Tuple{v} " " And{e}, Not{e} lookahead " " Delimited{e, sep}, Delimited1{e, sep} e separated by sep. The value is Tuple{v, ...} " " Same{} succeeds when the next token is on the same line " " Act{e, f} applies f to the value of e. f is an expression evaluated by _define_rule " " Prev{} the previous definition of the rule (only as an alternative of _define_rule) " " Fail{} never matches " " Results of rules are memoized for each position. " " Direct left recursion (sum ::= sum \"+\" term | term) is handled by growing the seed. "
