4. Libraries

The libraries in planck/lib are also written in planck and loaded by use("name"). They are listed in Libraries.

An operator cannot be passed as a function, so write a lambda such as (a, b) -> a + b.

for (p in xs) body matches the elements of a list or a vector against the pattern p.

Tables and vectors can also be read and written by t[key] and v[i] (chapter 10).
