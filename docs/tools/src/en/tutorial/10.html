10. Indexing and rewriting

a[i] and a[i] = v call _index_get and _index_set. These are functions joined by |, one for each kind of value, so a program can add its own kind.

Strings, lists, tuples and M-expressions are immutable, and writing to them stops the program.

Adding a kind

Put the new kind at the front of the fusion and pass the others to the original function.

Rewriting trees

t[p -> a | q -> b] is subst(t, ...). It tries each part of the tree from the root down, replaces a part that matches and does not try its inside. Repeat it until nothing changes.
